\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We ship a v0.1 draft. Five named limitations bound the scope of the
claims above. Carried forward verbatim from the project's narrative
report (NARRATIVE\_REPORT.md, §6) as the spine of honesty.

\paragraph{L1: Bag-of-tokens embedding cannot replicate the SRE from raw
stimuli.} \texttt{SelfModel.embed()} uses SHA1-hashed bag-of-tokens
embeddings. A forward-pass diagnostic confirmed that self-ref and
phonemic prompts both containing the trait word ``honest'' received
near-identical $\srel$, because the shared content word dominates the
embedding similarity over the framing distinction. The mechanical
SRE replication in E1 (\cref{sec:exp:e1}) sidesteps this by typing
$\srel$ per condition manually; the true forward-pass SRE replication
(E1b in \cref{sec:exp:future}) requires a sentence-transformer encoder
\cite{reimers2019sbert} or a BERT-class model. We expect this to land
the model in genuine SRE territory but cannot claim it from current
evidence.

\paragraph{L2: $\piself$ update rule is engineered, not derived.}
\Cref{eq:pi} expresses $\piself$ as a function of prediction-error
variance over a rolling window. The form is FEP-inspired but is not a
derivation from variational free energy first principles. A defensible
derivation would cast $\piself$ as the inverse variance of the
predictive distribution under a specified likelihood/prior pair and
recover \cref{eq:pi} as the natural-gradient update of that quantity.
We have not done this; reviewers will reasonably ask where the rule
comes from.

\paragraph{L3: No human-data fit.} All experiments are agent-internal.
We have not fit our model to Symons \& Johnson \cite{symons1997meta}
effect sizes, to Rogers, Kuiper \& Kirker \cite{rogers1977sre} raw
data, or to any clinical SRE-attenuation dataset. The
"matches human direction'' claim in E1 is qualitative, not
quantitative. The quantitative side-by-side against CMR3
\cite{cohen2022cmr3} (E5 in \cref{sec:exp:future}) is also not done;
CMR3 is not packaged as a library and reimplementation is a
multi-day effort we have not budgeted.

\paragraph{L4: Deterministic experiments without noise.} All three
experiments are deterministic given seed; cell-level variance is exactly
zero in E1. Adding realistic encoding noise --- Gaussian on forget\_score,
or stochastic embedding perturbation --- is necessary for honest variance
estimates and for the OLS regressions in E2 to be applied to data with
the variance structure they assume. We expect the qualitative
conclusions to be robust but cannot claim it from current evidence.

\paragraph{L5: Retrieval uses memory embedding, not encoding-time
$\muself$.} The retrieval ranking (\cref{eq:rank}) computes
$\cos(e_i,\muself^{now})$ between the memory's content embedding and
the agent's \emph{current} $\muself$, falling back to the stored
$\srel_i$ snapshot. The proposal sketch in the project's idea report
specified ranking by similarity between current $\muself$ and the
memory's $\muself^{\text{enc}}$ --- a strictly more faithful
match to encoding-specificity \cite{tulving1973encodingspec}. Adding
$\muself^{\text{enc}}$ persistence is a small change but
not yet done.

\paragraph{Path forward.}
L1 (sentence-transformers) and L3 (CMR3 fit) are the two changes
that would most strongly upgrade the present account from "framework
demonstration" to "submission-ready empirical paper". L2 (precision
derivation) and L5 (encoding-time $\muself$) are smaller surface fixes.
L4 (noise) is a re-run of the existing experiments with a single
hyperparameter added. None of these change the central mechanism;
they sharpen its quantitative claims.
